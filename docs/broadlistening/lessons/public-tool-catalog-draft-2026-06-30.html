Broad Listening Tool Catalog (Draft)

Translated from the Japanese original: wiki/analyses/public-tool-catalog-draft-2026-06-30.md (commit 3459fc6). Machine-translated by Gemini and not yet reviewed by NISHIO Hirokazu.

Summary: A tool catalog draft to explain Kouchou-AI (広聴AI) / Idobata (いどばた) / Cartographer / Jigsaw Sensemaker / tttc-light-js to the outside world without confusion. It will be repurposed for the #564 public case page and the August 2nd tech/tool materials.

Conclusion

For the #564 public case page and the August 2nd tech/tool materials, it is safer to divide broad listening into six layers: collect / deepen / analyze / show / classify / read-and-act, rather than trying to explain it in a single word. Even in the meeting minutes, Kouchou-AI, Idobata, Cartographer, Jigsaw Sensemaker, and tttc-light-js appear to be in the same ecosystem, but their primary inputs, deliverables, and reader expectations are different. From meeting-cartographer-idobata-boundary-2026-06-30

The shortest outward-facing explanation should position Kouchou-AI as "an OSS that organizes collected free-text descriptions and makes it easier to find issues," and present Idobata / Cartographer on the collection/deepening side, with Jigsaw Sensemaker / tttc-light-js added as alternative routes for direct LLM classification or TTTC lineage. This avoids the misreading that Kouchou-AI automatically handles everything from collection, dialogue, conflict mediation, to policy decisions. From report-reading-guide-minimum-wording-2026-06-30

Public Tool Catalog Draft

layer reader question primary tool / route say this avoid saying
collect How to collect voices Questionnaires, SNS, in-person records, public comments, existing data, input plugins as needed Kouchou-AI imports collected free-text descriptions into a manageable form for analysis. The entire design of opinion collection is completed solely by Kouchou-AI.
deepen How to listen deeply idobata / Cartographer Idobata-style tools are on the side of deepening opinions through 1-on-1 dialogue and follow-up questions. Idobata / Cartographer are standard features of Kouchou-AI.
analyze How to view the overall structure kouchou-ai Kouchou-AI extracts, clusters, and labels pre-collected free-text descriptions, making it easier to find issues. It guarantees proof of majority, statistical public opinion, or correctness of policy decisions.
show How to share and verify public viewer / report / individual opinions It allows returning from the overall view to individual opinions, enabling humans to verify the AI's summary. The visual appearance of the diagram directly represents analysis quality or representativeness.
classify How to classify into different structures LLM grouping, jigsaw-sensemaker, tttc-light-js Direct LLM classification and tree-native routes are being considered as analytical styles distinct from scatter plots. Equating Jigsaw or tttc-light-js with the Kouchou-AI current UI.
read-and-act How to connect to decisions Human interpretation, additional research, policy/operational decisions The report is supplementary material for discovering issues and grasping points of contention, serving as a basis for deciding what to investigate next. AI single-handedly determines public will, conclusions, or consensus building.

Drop-in Copy

Short public copy

Kouchou-AI is an OSS that organizes collected free-text opinions using LLMs and clustering, making it easier to discover issues and points of contention. It is used to read voices gathered from questionnaires, public comments, SNS, in-person records, etc., while navigating back and forth between the overall picture and individual opinions.

However, Kouchou-AI does not automate statistical public opinion polls or policy decisions. Since the results depend on the target data and how it was collected, for important decisions, they must be read in conjunction with the collection method, original data, context, and human verification. From report-reading-guide-minimum-wording-2026-06-30

Ecosystem copy

In the stage of collecting or deepening voices, aspects such as questionnaire design, in-person records, interactive tools like Idobata, and follow-up questions/comprehension aids like Cartographer are involved. Kouchou-AI takes on the role in the subsequent stage, analyzing and visualizing the collected free-text descriptions, and making them easy to read as a public viewer or report. From meeting-cartographer-idobata-boundary-2026-06-30

Direct LLM classification / TTTC-style routes like Jigsaw Sensemaker and tttc-light-js serve as references for analytical styles distinct from the scatter plot-centric Kouchou-AI. In particular, conflict axis discovery and tree-native organization are important next themes, but they are not promised as default capabilities of the current Kouchou-AI. From jigsaw-sensemaker, from talk-to-the-city

From public-case-page-skeleton-2026-06-30

The tool_lineage of the case list distinguishes at least the following values:

  • kouchou-ai: Kouchou-AI confirmed / public artifact available
  • Talk to the City lineage: TTTC / tttc-light-js / derived from TTTC
  • broad listening adjacent: broad listening in a broader sense / AI-assisted citizen dialogue
  • idobata / dialogue: collection, deepening, and proposal generation side
  • enterprise / VOC: enterprise / Voice of Customer analysis
  • candidate: primary public source / publication permission / tool lineage is unconfirmed

Without this classification, broad listening official cases in a broader sense like Osaka Prefecture, policy formation cases involving multiple tools like Team Mirai, TTTC lineage cases like DirectVote, and enterprise / VOC cases like Cybozu / Altius Link would all be misread as "Kouchou-AI implementation track records." From public-case-page-skeleton-2026-06-30

What Not To Do

  • Do not use Kouchou-AI as a "generic term for overall broad listening."
  • Do not list Idobata / Cartographer / Jigsaw Sensemaker / tttc-light-js as current product capabilities of Kouchou-AI.
  • Do not promise conflict axis discovery as a default output of the current Kouchou-AI.
  • Do not display broad listening in a broader sense / TTTC lineage / enterprise VOC / candidate collectively as Kouchou-AI usage track records on the public case page.
  • Do not use Slack-only / Drive-only cases or screenshots with unconfirmed permissions as the basis for a public page.
