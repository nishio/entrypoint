A/B Experiment on Top-Level Label Refinement and Misinterpretation of Length Direction

Translated from the Japanese original: wiki/analyses/labelling-prompt-few-shot-template-confound-2026-06-03.md (commit 3459fc6). Machine-translated by Gemini and not yet reviewed by NISHIO Hirokazu.

Summary: As a result of running 7 items of human preference A/B (none vs setwise_refine) on the 2026-06-02 LLM grouping 400-item corpus with label_only, setwise_refine was the winner in 7/7 cases. Initially, this result was organized as a verbosity confound, but a reinvestigation on 6/8 and 6/9 revealed that I had conversely misidentified "which one is more verbose". In fact, refine_none (= no top-level refinement = merge_labels as is) is verbose, and refine_setwise (sibling-aware refinement) is shorter. Therefore, the v1 result captured "refinement on > refinement off", and the exact structure is that the verbosity reduction effect and the sibling-awareness effect are mixed within the refinement. The templating issue of the MERGE_LABELLING_PROMPT few-shot remains valid.

Major Correction on 2026-06-09: The first edition on 2026-06-03 and the correction on 2026-06-04 of this page mistakenly reversed the direction of verbose/concise for refine_none and refine_setwise. Reinvestigation showed that in reality, refine_none (no refinement = merge_labels as is) is verbose, and refine_setwise (sibling-aware refinement) is shorter, and the user's "the shorter candidate wins 7/7" was actually "refine_setwise wins 7/7". Therefore, the framing of "verbosity confound" itself was incorrect, and the v1 result actually captured a meaningful signal of "refinement on > refinement off". See the "Correction on 2026-06-09" section below for details. The old text of this page is kept for history.

Correction on 2026-06-09

Correct length ordering

Confirmed on cluster 1_1:

labelling_run label Length
refine_none AI技術による顧客体験の向上と業務効率化の推進 Long
refine_setwise AIによる顧客体験と業務効率の向上 Medium
refine_short AIによる顧客体験向上 Short

The refine_none mode is a mode that does not execute top-level refinement, and adopts the output of the merge_labels stage as it is as the top-level label. Because merge_labels is strongly guided by the MERGE_LABELLING_PROMPT (few-shot example AI技術の導入による意見分析の効率化への期待) and converges to the AI技術による…の推進 form, the labels of refine_none are verbose.

refine_setwise is a process of sibling-aware rewriting, and as a result, it organizes verbose merge_labels. Although there is no length constraint, rewriting to differentiate from siblings naturally works in the direction of reducing verbosity.

refine_short further adds max_label_length=18 to shrink it even shorter.

Correct interpretation of v1 results

nishio's 7 answers to the v1 bundle (none vs setwise_refine) of human-pairwise-label-preference-experiment-2026-06-02 are:

  • refine_setwise was the winner in 7/7, all with confidence 3
  • It is true that "the shorter candidate wins", but that "shorter candidate" is refine_setwise, and the "longer candidate" is refine_none
  • nishio's free text "For the others, shorter is generally better" is correct in the sense of "I prefer shorter", but that preference is identical to the preference for refine_setwise

In other words, the v1 result shows "sibling-aware refinement (setwise) > no refinement (none)" in 7/7 cases.

This is a meaningful signal, but not a pure sibling-awareness effect:

  • In refinement on vs off, both (a) sibling-awareness and (b) the effect of rewriting the verbose expression of merge_labels are operating simultaneously
  • It is impossible to isolate whether nishio's preference is (a) or (b) in this comparison

Policy for restarting (after re-re-correction)

"Verbosity confound" was an incorrect framing, so it is withdrawn. Instead, the following:

Path A: Fix the templating of MERGE_LABELLING_PROMPT few-shot (current main)

This remains valid. The refine_none mode is verbose because the MERGE_LABELLING_PROMPT few-shot example "AI技術の導入による意見分析の効率化への期待" bakes in the 〜による〜の〜 syntax. Making the few-shot topic-neutral / syntax-diverse will improve the output of refine_none, which in turn improves the input to refine_setwise.

Specific correction proposals (A1 + A2 are realistic):

  • A1. Make the example topic-neutral: Remove the 〜による〜の〜 form from the output examples and replace it with a noun phrase style starting with a content word
  • A2. Explicit prohibition: Add "avoid boilerplate syntax like 〜による〜の〜" and "use short noun phrases" to the prompt body
  • A3. Diverse few-shots: Line up multiple examples with different syntax
  • A4. Zero-shot: Discard the example and use only instructions
Path B (Old): default length constraint on setwise_refine → Withdrawn

This path was proposed because the old analysis mistakenly assumed that "setwise_refine is elaborating verbosely". In reality, setwise_refine is already rewriting in a shorter direction, so a default length constraint is unnecessary. It is sufficient to keep refine_short as a separate mode.

Path D (New): Set up a comparison to measure the isolated effect of sibling-awareness

Since the v1 result (setwise > none) is a combined effect of refinement on/off, measuring the pure sibling-awareness effect requires "sibling-aware refinement vs non-sibling-aware refinement (both about the same length)".

The refine_balanced / refine_contrast modes exist in the existing artifacts, but it is unconfirmed what these modes do. I will confirm that first before designing the A/B.

Path C (Old): Reconfigure with none vs setwise_refine_short → Rejected

In the old analysis, I thought this would be a length control, but in reality, none (long) vs short (shortest) widens the length gap even further. It does not improve the confound and is rejected.

Old Analysis Text (For Reference)

The text is left as is for those who want to compare the old framing and the corrected version. Because it contains content with a direction misidentification, read it by cross-referencing with the correction above.

(Old) Conclusion

The comparison of factor_under_test=labelling_process (none vs setwise) on the 2026-06-02 corpus was designed such that the isolated effect of labelling_process could not be measured because two independent issues were confounded. The experiment is temporarily reset and not used as material for adoption decisions.

The two issues (details below):

  1. INITIAL/MERGE labelling few-shot template: It bakes the 〜による〜の〜 syntax and the "AIによる" prefix into all labelling outputs ← Issue 1 itself is valid, but the target it bakes into is the labels of the refine_none mode (= merge_labels)
  2. No length constraint on setwise_refine: The refinement step elaborates and rewrites into a long form like "AI技術による…の推進" ← It was reversed. setwise_refine is the one that shortens

(Old) Observation (with direction misidentification)

The "shorter candidate" was the winner in 7/7 cases, and all confidences were 3 (high). All 7 winning cases were short noun phrases starting with AIによる…, and all 7 losing cases were a somewhat long prefix + action noun like AI技術による…の推進 / …への取り組み

The factual relationship (the shape of the label) is correct. The error was in the direction assuming "short AIによる… = refine_none, long AI技術による…の推進 = refine_setwise", whereas the reality is the opposite.

(Old) Explanation of "elaboration" for Issue 2 → Withdrawn

The old version's "setwise_refine elaborates the base label like AI技術による…の推進" is incorrect. The reality is that the base merge_labels is in the form of AI技術による…の推進, and setwise_refine rewrites it shorter.

Generalized rules (extracted from this case)

Aside from the direction misidentification issue, the following two general rules can be extracted from this entire case:

  1. Spot-check the actual output of both candidates before conducting an A/B evaluation. Do not infer style from the candidate name (none/setwise/short), but directly check the artifact CSV. I failed to do this and mistook the direction.
  2. The few-shot example becomes the ceiling of label quality. AI技術の導入による意見分析の効率化への期待 in the MERGE_LABELLING_PROMPT strongly guides the syntax, making the merge_labels output without refinement verbose. In labelling quality improvement, starting by reviewing the prompt example yields a greater return than process / sibling-awareness.

Open Questions

  • What kind of refinement do the refine_balanced / refine_contrast modes do? Can they be used for an A/B measuring the isolated effect of sibling-awareness? → Confirmed on 2026-06-09, all 4 are sibling-aware variants and a non-sibling-aware one is absent. To isolate sibling-awareness alone, a new mode (D-3) is required
  • When the MERGE_LABELLING_PROMPT few-shot is changed to be topic-neutral, will only the refine_none output improve, or will the refine_setwise output also follow and improve?
  • The v1 result (setwise > none) is the effect of refinement on/off, but is it appropriate to use this as an "adoption rationale", or should it be kept strictly exploratory?
  • For the result of v2 (setwise vs short), the only factor is length cap on/off, but will nishio's v1 free text "too short and loses too much information, which is a problem" reproduce in cluster 1_8, or is the 18 cap acceptable across all clusters?
