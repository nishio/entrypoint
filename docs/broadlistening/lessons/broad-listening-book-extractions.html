Insights from the Book for Future Development Decisions

Translated from the Japanese original: wiki/analyses/broad-listening-book-extractions.md (commit 3459fc6). Machine-translated by Gemini and not yet reviewed by NISHIO Hirokazu.

Summary: Extracts insights from the book that influence future development decisions. Separates design intentions, empirical values, and verification results, treating the improvement of labeling quality with the KJ method prompt as an unverified hypothesis.

Read book source and extract only the insights that are effective for future development. Deviating from the book's own purpose (explaining to a broad readership), we select items that people who touch the code should know.

1. "Publishable" backing of claims already in this wiki

The following claims, previously written in the wiki based on Slack / meeting notes / individual observations, were organized into a publishable form in Chapter 13 of the book. If asked "why this design?" in future PR reviews, we can cite the book.

However, "written in the book" does not mean "verified." What Chapter 13 describes are mostly design intentions and empirical observations, not results of effect verification. Whether each item is a "verified effect," "empirical value," or "unverified hypothesis" is clarified below.

  • The reason for choosing K-means is not accuracy but "agreement between scatter plot and intuition" (10_00, 13.2.4). Includes a figure showing "isolated islands" generated by Spectral Clustering observed in the scatter plot of the NTV 2024 lower house election special program. → Reinforces "why UMAP → clustering" in pipeline
  • 13.2.4 explicitly admits that clustering using 2D results of UMAP is a compromise different from standard practice. It cites the design decision to "match the scatter plot and the visual appearance of the clustering" and the empirical observation that accuracy improvement is limited because information is already compressed after free-text extraction, as two grounds.
  • The exponent of ∛n / n^(2/3) is not a theoretical optimum but an empirical value (13.4.2). A value settled on after trial and error based on visibility criteria. The book itself admits that there are situations in practical use where the number of clusters should be increased more. → Serves as a supplement to auto-cluster-defaults
  • [Unverified] The hypothesis that labeling quality "should" improve by using Japanese specialized terms like KJ method (KJ法) / nameplate (表札) (13.2.6). The book's description is a design intention, not a verification result. The comparative experiment issue #882 (created 2026-05-29) explicitly states "it has not yet been isolated as a comparative experiment whether including KJ method-like words in the prompt truly improves label quality," and as of 2026-09-15, it is OPEN, 0 comments, no related PR = experiment unexecuted. Therefore, this should not be cited as "grounds for practice." → kj-method-broadlistening-framing-2026-05-25
  • Reasons for not adopting automatic optimization such as silhouette score (13.4.2 footnote): It is more practical if users can choose the "granularity they want to see," and automatic optimization does not always return the "desirable number of clusters." → Reinforces pipeline and umap-seed-history

2. Specific obstacles / requests repeated in field operations (separating known issues and unaddressed ones)

Already visible / partially addressed

  • Labels are too abstract (Asahi Shimbun 04_05, Tokyo 05) — The operation of editors rewriting them by hand had become the norm. Asahi Shimbun's countermeasure was modifying the prompt to "give 5 headings." On the Kouchou-AI (広聴AI) side, "Please include specific events and actions" and "Avoid abstract expressions" are written into the prompt (13.2.7), but the reality remains that operators re-label them by hand.
  • The dilemma of the number of clusters: If few, it's abstract; if many, it can't be fully conveyed to readers (Asahi Shimbun 04_05 and Tokyo 05 both want to see details at 40-50 clusters). The ∛n default is based on a "good-looking figure." Presets leaning towards practical use also seem necessary.
  • source-link / traceability was added in the Team Mirai version and returned to the mainstream (10_00). The design importance of being able to "return to the original utterance without swallowing the AI output whole."

Unaddressed (≈ Development opportunity)

  • The off-topic large cluster problem (Asahi Shimbun 04_05, Tokyo 05) — Because "all opinions are put on the scatter plot," opinions off-theme become the largest cluster and cannot be handled in reporting, a complaint raised from multiple field sites. Asahi explicitly requested an "outlier exclusion function." Kouchou-AI has "dense cluster extraction," but this is a "density-based cutoff" and not a "theme-relevance cutoff." A function to filter during extraction by on/off-topic judgment by LLM appears unimplemented.
  • Keyword design for SNS data acquisition is the most difficult (Asahi Shimbun 04_05) — A problem before Broad Listening, work that the field reinvents in Kouchou-AI use cases dealing with SNS data. Templating X / YouTube acquisition and keyword design or turning them into an input plugin (plugin-system) is an opportunity.
  • UI capable of local execution is sought on the news organization side (Asahi Shimbun 04_05) — The OpenAI API can be used under a security contract, but "uploading data to another company's server" is a high hurdle. A perspective that perfectly matches the Kouchou-AI self-hosting requirement for municipalities. Grounds for why LocalLLM support (PR #824 etc.) should be continuously emphasized.
  • A clear difference in clustering quality between surveys vs SNS (Asahi Shimbun 04_05) — Those from surveys with a fixed certain direction are clean, SNS is messy. Room for design declaring the "degree of orderliness of the assumed distribution" in input plugin / config.

3. Areas not in the existing implementation that the book indicates it will "touch next"

Separation of pros and cons (13.4.3)

  • Approach 1: Adding a sentiment dimension — LLM estimates a score of -1.0 to +1.0 during extraction, concatenating it to the embedding with weight. There is a figure showing a wind power generation cluster separated into pros and cons when weight=0.75 for about 1,000 public comments on power policy. A tradeoff is noted that if the weight is too large, topic similarity disappears.
  • Approach 2: DivCon (Divide and Conciliate) — Non-use of embeddings. Topic discovery by LLM → Opinion classification → Discovery of conflict axes → Anchor generation → Scoring. Includes an output example of a "conflict view" where hardline opinions line up at the top.

As a development decision, these align with branches of the analysis_mode=llm_grouping system (pr-827-llm-grouping-capabilities-plan-2026-05-18). The book only writes it as a future research theme, but the plugin / analysis_mode design of Kouchou-AI is becoming a structure that can encompass this prototype.

Options for Long Context architecture (13.5)

  • The historical background is that initially only 4,096 tokens could fit, leading to the embedding + UMAP configuration. Now that 1 million tokens is standard, architectures that "pass everything to the LLM" like TTTC Turbo / LLM direct grouping / Fujitsu-central ministries demonstration experiment have also entered practical range.
  • Pros and cons of scatter plot type and long context type:
    • Scatter plot type — Automatically generates a "map of opinions," intuitive, but accuracy is poor due to clustering after 2D compression.
    • Long context type — Strong in pros/cons separation and nuance distinction, high clustering accuracy, but does not output a good-looking scatter plot.
  • The parallel running (mode switching) of both types is explicitly stated as "ideal" by the book as well. → Backs the analysis_mode strategy of pipeline.

4. Meta-insight on "consensus building" design

column/Things I noticed after collecting 10,000 voices (Aoyama Shutaro) is a point that overturns the premise Kouchou-AI implicitly relies on. Summary:

Collected 9,688 proposals. Answered nearly 10,000 questions. If asked whether they became materials for interest coordination at the root of political decision-making, to be honest, they did not. Many of what was collected remained "fragmentary declarations of position."

  • There is a gap between "delivering voices" and "connecting to decision-making"
  • The bottleneck of consensus building is not scale but self-understanding. Even a 3-person internal meeting conflicts due to lack of self-understanding.
  • The strategy shifts from "scale-up" (aggregating 100 million people's opinions) to "scale-out" (the same quality of dialogue across multiple field sites).
  • The Ota City personalization meeting adopted a structure of delving into self-understanding through AI dialogue beforehand, before entering a 20-person resident meeting.

Implications for development:

  • Kouchou-AI concentrates downstream on "collecting / classifying / visualizing," and is effective for decision-making only when combined with upstream self-understanding support (interview-based products like idobata (いどばた)).
  • Room to hold an index measuring the quality of collected opinions (degree of fragmentation / degree of separation of position and reason) as a meta-column in args.csv. This can be implemented with Structured Output from 12.4.8.
  • The inherent value of the scatter plot UX (column/Safe empathy) = observation from an irrefutable distance = echo chamber antidote, which is also the ground reinforcing the reason for the UI's existence.
