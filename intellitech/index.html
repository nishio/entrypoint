How do you learn, organize and output? This book describes intellitechs (methods to boost your intellectual creativity) using specific examples of programming and writing books.

You can not write a useful program for work by just copying sone sample codes. Likewise, intellitech doesn't work well for you by just copying from books. In this book, I compare numerous intellitechs. Through the comparison, you will understand the important principle, achieve the ability to modify, combine and create new methods for your situation.
